AI Health Application

Derma

An AI-powered skin analysis and product recommender that examines your skin in real time through your webcam and recommends skincare formulations tailored specifically to you, then finds real matching products with images.

Source Code Demo Video

Overview

Derma looks at your skin in real time through your webcam and recommends skincare formulations tailored specifically to you. Take a photo straight from your browser using your webcam, no need to upload anything. The photo gets analysed by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite, which works out your skin type, your main concerns, and which ingredients would actually help.

From there it generates personalised skincare formulation recommendations based on that analysis. It then searches for real products that match those recommendations, and pulls in product images using Cheerio HTML parsing so you are not just looking at a list of text. The result is a complete, visual skincare recommendation engine that runs entirely in the browser and Node.js backend.

Key Features

Webcam Capture

Take a photo straight from your browser using the WebRTC API. No file uploads needed, just allow camera access and snap a picture.

AI Skin Analysis

Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite analyses your skin photo to determine skin type, main concerns, and which ingredients would actually help.

Personalised Recommendations

Generates personalised skincare formulation recommendations based on the AI analysis of your specific skin type and concerns.

Real Product Search

Searches for real products matching the recommendations and pulls in product images using Cheerio HTML parsing for visual results.

Ingredient Matching

Identifies which specific ingredients would help with your skin concerns and matches them against actual product formulations.

Browser-Based

The entire experience runs in the browser with a Node.js backend. No native app install required, works on any modern browser with camera access.

How It Works

Capture

Allow webcam access and take a photo of your face straight from the browser. The image is captured using the browser's WebRTC API and sent to the backend.

Analyse

The image gets sent to Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite, which identifies your skin type and concerns. Gemini returns a list of recommended formulations and ingredients based on what it found.

Match Products

Cheerio parses the HTML of a skincare product website to find matching products based on the recommended ingredients and pull their images for visual display.

Display Results

Matched products with images are displayed alongside your personalised recommendations, giving you a complete visual guide to products that suit your skin.

Engineering Challenges

Web Scraping Behind Cloudflare

The product search scrapes Care to Beauty for real product images. The site uses Cloudflare CDN which blocks requests from datacenter IPs, meaning the scraper works locally but may fail when deployed to cloud services. The fix involves routing through a scraping proxy for production deployments.

Deeply Nested JSON Extraction

The original regex used a non-greedy pattern to extract product JSON from the page, but because the JSON is deeply nested, the regex stopped at the first closing brace inside the object. Fixed by replacing the regex with a brace-depth counter that walks the string and finds the actual closing brace of the JSON object.
